# Storyboard — AI Native Vision Agents

## Narrative spine

The film avoids presenting isolated demos. It frames vision agents as one reusable intelligence fabric that travels across industries and converts cameras into coordinated operating action.

| Chapter | Industry | Core message | Visual source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enterprise | Cameras are passive; vision agents reason | NX hero footage |
| 2 | Cross-industry | One fabric across plants, fleets, ports and airports | Fleet aerial |
| 3–4 | Manufacturing | Predict risk and structure evidence | Plant safety feed |
| 5 | Fleet | Coach unsafe driving in the moment | Driver dashcam |
| 6 | Warehouse | Activate existing CCTV | Warehouse vision demo |
| 7 | Control tower | Convert alerts into accountable action | Operations UI |
| 8–9 | Port | Track movement and protect people near equipment | Port yard and operator footage |
| 10–11 | Airport | Predict queues and protect baggage handoffs | Passenger and baggage footage |
| 12 | Command | Prioritised events, recommendations and approvals | Airport command centre |
| 13 | Close | One reusable, governed vision-agent fabric | Aircraft landing |

## Editing treatment

- 16:9, 1280×720, 24 fps.
- 0.45-second cross-fades and directional transitions.
- Consistent AI HUD, industry labels, scan line and detection boxes.
- Low-volume source sound under narration.
- Closed captions and chapter timeline generated with each render.
- Final video compressed below the GitHub browser upload threshold.
